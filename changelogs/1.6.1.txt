BotC v1.6.1 - XX XX, 2026

Changes and features:
- Updated to Minecraft 26.3
- Finished the upstairs interior of the Inn.
- Added several new trees around the build.
- Added Rinku mod to enable FM's browser feature.
- Added Default Options mod to handle keybinds better.
- Added a HUD element to display the current number of votes as well as the required majority.
- Added a nice little bug to the Report Modpack Bugs button.
- Added a Discord button to the Multiplayer menu.
- Added a "Clocktower Items" creative tab to quickly grab copies of items that can normally only be spawned in.
- Redid the Storyteller HUD element for managing an active nomination.
- Redid the Modpack Credits screen to scroll and look a bit nicer.
- Character tokens in the bag menu now get slightly larger when they are selected.
- Reduced the volume of most custom sounds to be consistent with each other and with Minecraft sounds.
- Changed the countdown sound.
- Changed the vote sound to use the old countdown sound.
- When a player receives a role, it now automatically appears on their spot in the Grimoire.
- Vote items can no longer be removed from your offhand.

Technical changes:
- The pack now uses AnyBind instead of ChatBinds since the latter wasn't being updated. This means that keybinds can't be sent as a text component since this mod doesn't allow it. Happy to hear alternatives!
- Added a section in set_grim_roles.mcfunction to show a player's own role in their Grimoire upon reveal. Homebrew tools may need to update for this.
